# VRI-STR-007
# The Content Flywheel

Status: Active

## Authority

Internal Veridion strategy document establishing the minimum reusable outputs expected from every research effort.

## Intent

To amortize the high cost of regulatory research across many downstream uses so that institutional knowledge compounds rather than remaining trapped in single deliverables.

## Plain Language Summary

Research is expensive. Publishing the same insight in multiple forms is inexpensive. Never research for only one output.

## Operational Meaning

When a research session concludes, the producer maps the work against the minimum outputs list and generates every applicable asset. The resulting nodes are linked according to Atomic Knowledge Architecture rules.

## Enforcement Considerations

Research that produces only a client memo or a single notebook is incomplete under this principle. Reviewers should request the missing flywheel outputs or an explicit justification for why they do not apply.

## Common Failure Points

- One-off client research that never re-enters the knowledge bank
- Updating a Reg Bible without also updating related Context or Action Maps
- Writing an FAQ that is not linked back to its source regulation node
- Treating newsletter content as ephemeral rather than derived from durable nodes

## Principle

Research is expensive.

Publishing is inexpensive.

Every research effort must produce multiple reusable assets.

## Minimum Outputs

Every completed research session SHOULD generate as many of the following as applicable:

- Reg Bible update
- Reg & Pol Notebook
- Context Map
- Action Map
- Playbook update
- FAQ
- Glossary entry
- Penumbrant Paper
- Client Advisory
- Newsletter content
- AI Knowledge Node
