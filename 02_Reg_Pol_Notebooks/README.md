# 02_Reg_Pol_Notebooks

## Purpose

Policy analysis layer — legislative history, stakeholder maps, current debates, and the political economy behind the rules. These notebooks explain *why* the regulation looks the way it does and who is still contesting it.

## Contents

Scaffolding stage (2026-07-30).

## Relationships

- Upstream: Primary legislative and agency sources; Reg Bibles
- Downstream: Penumbrant Papers (policy conflict often creates gray zones), Context Maps
- Related: Public product site Reg & Pol offerings

## Contribution Expectations

- Neutral analysis of competing interests
- Explicit linkage to the statutory nodes that result from the policy process
- Avoid advocacy language; document positions and power dynamics
